XFreq: Geometry-Aligned Cross-View Residual Learning for Sparse 3D Ultrasound Reconstruction

首次收录：2026-11-24 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-23 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

Three-dimensional (3D) ultrasound provides valuable volumetric context for anatomical assessment, motivating reconstruction from widely available two-dimensional (2D) slices. Sparse slices leave much of the volume unobserved, even when their acquisition poses are known. Existing methods interpolate observed intensities or incorporate learned 2D image features into 3D reconstruction, but their complementary roles in predicting smooth structure and residual details remain less explored. We introduce XFreq, a geometry-aligned framework for sparse 3D ultrasound reconstruction. It uses known slice poses and finite footprints to query 2D image features at 3D locations, complementing splatted intensities and sampling geometry. A low-frequency decoder predicts a smooth 3D field from the splat representation, while a high-frequency decoder additionally incorporates the queried 2D features to predict residual details. A learned gating module regulates the residual contribution based on geometry and cross-slice feature variation.
