DraftKV: Protecting Persistent Worlds through Hierarchical Streaming Retrieval

首次收录：2026-11-25 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-24 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

Interactive video world models enable action- and camera-controlled exploration, yet struggle to maintain consistent spatial geometry and appearance when revisiting previously explored locations. Historical KV caches help auto-regressive video diffusion models maintain long-term consistency, but their retrieval and attention computation introduce substantial latency as history grows. We propose DraftKV, a training-free framework that retrieves historical KV caches based on geometric grounding and query-conditioned relevance, while combining CPU offloading with tile-wise streaming attention to reduce GPU memory usage and inference latency. Theoretically, we show that draft relevance reproduces the mean branch of full-resolution block relevance exactly, so its deviation is confined to the $β$-weighted max branch, and we bound retrieval utility loss in terms of the measured Top-K selection overlap. We further show that draft relevance cannot distinguish blocks with similar pooled descriptors regardless of camera pose, motivating geometric grounding before refinement.
