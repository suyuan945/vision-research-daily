摘要（arXiv 元数据）

Visual proxies decouple explicit world-state simulation from photorealistic video synthesis, offering a controllable interface for both offline clip creation and real-time interactive streaming. Yet a practical renderer must address two challenges. Upstream proxies vary from coarse layouts to fine articulated geometry depending on the interaction, and streaming inference must operate in a few causal steps without accumulating drift across self-generated history. We present Invoker-Render, pairing a bidirectional creation model with a causal four-step streaming model under a shared conditioning interface over structural proxies, an appearance reference, and language. Trained on an aligned approximately 200-hour multi-granularity corpus, Invoker-Render honors fine geometric constraints when supplied while synthesizing plausible local motion under coarse proxies, enabling runtime selection of input detail. To bridge bidirectional creation and real-time streaming without compounding step compression and rollout drift, we introduce Progressive Forcing (ProForcing).
