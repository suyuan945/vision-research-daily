VG3R: Video Generation Model for Feed-Forward 3D Reconstruction

首次收录：2026-11-24 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-21 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

Video generation models are widely used as 3D priors for novel view synthesis and 3D scene generation. Fine-tuned for perception, generation models also excel at dense prediction such as depth estimation. However, methods that apply video generation models to 3D reconstruction either predict depth without cameras or generate geometry and estimate cameras through many iterative denoising steps, which makes them slower. In this paper, we present $\textbf{VG3R}$, which turns a pretrained video generation model into a feed-forward 3D reconstruction model. Our key observation is that such a model already captures the depth and camera motion of a video, but not in the form required for reconstruction. We introduce two components: $\textit{clip-level shift prediction}$, which corrects depth distortion caused by the unknown shift in decoded inverse depth, and a $\textit{multi-layer camera head}$, which predicts per-frame cameras parameters by combining features across transformer blocks. Using Wan2.1 as its video backbone, VG3R reconstructs a scene and estimate the camera of every frame without iterative denoising.
