PaveSplat: Resolution-Robust Feed-Forward Gaussian Splatting with Residually Corrected Surface-Aligned Covariances

首次收录：2026-11-22 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-19 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

高斯渲染
arXiv 原文 ↗

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

We present PaveSplat, a feed-forward Gaussian splatting method that adds a learned residual to the analytic surface-aligned covariance, keeping surfels paving the visible surface across rendering resolutions. Pixel-aligned feed-forward Gaussian splatting predicts one primitive per pixel from a few images in a single pass, and its rendering quality depends on how well these primitives cover the surface. Recent surfel-based methods compute the surface-aligned covariance analytically from predicted depth. However, this covariance has no way to correct the surfel orientation where the depth-derived frame is ill-posed. The surfels then cover the surface loosely; at the training resolution each surfel's one-pixel minimum footprint covers the gaps, but they show up as holes when the same prediction is re-rendered at a higher resolution. To address this, we keep the analytic covariance as an initialization, with its axes aligned to the surface tangents, and add an identity-initialized unit-quaternion residual that reorients a surfel only where this improves rendering, turning surfels that the analytic frame mis-aims back onto the surface.
