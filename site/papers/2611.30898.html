World Models as Native Latent Action Models

首次收录：2026-11-24 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-23 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

世界模型视频生成多模态 / 具身视觉

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

Large and diverse video sources, such as egocentric human video and heterogeneous robot recordings, carry rich dynamics but come either without actions or without a shared action space. Latent action models (LAMs) can recover these dynamics, but they train their inverse- and forward-dynamics models (IDM, FDM) from scratch, trading off pixel reconstruction against latent capacity and accuracy. Pretrained video world models natively generate high-fidelity dynamics, yet represent them densely, entangled across all layers. Viewing the world model as a LAM, we show that these dynamics can be extracted with minimal changes to the model. Native LAM operates one pretrained video diffusion transformer as both IDM and FDM, with learnable dynamics registers as the only causal bridge between the two, and trains on counterfactual temporal pairs that differ in motion alone, so that the registers distill a video's dynamics into a compact native latent action.
