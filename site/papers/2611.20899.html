SciWorldBench: Do Video World Models Really Understand Scientific Experiments?

首次收录：2026-11-18 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-16 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

While the ability of video generation models to accurately depict scientific phenomena has received growing attention, determining whether these models genuinely understand the underlying principles remains a critical challenge. Existing benchmarks typically evaluate video samples in isolation, making it difficult to expose models that simply fit superficial visual priors. In scientific experiments, a small change in one condition produces a markedly different outcome even when the rest of the setup remains fixed. Capitalizing on this sensitivity, we introduce SciWorldBench, a benchmark comprising paired scientific experiments. Each pair varies a single condition in an otherwise fixed setup, yielding two mutually exclusive expected outcomes. Each generation prompt specifies the condition but omits the expected outcome. We evaluate ten text-and-image-to-video (TI2V) models using an independent vision-language model (VLM) that assesses each video without access to the condition text or the label identifying the correct outcome.
