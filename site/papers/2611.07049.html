Gaussians Can Subtract: Neural Dissolve Fields for Hybrid Scene Rendering

首次收录：2026-11-09 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-04 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

三维几何
arXiv 原文 ↗

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

Hybrid Mesh-Gaussian rendering combines compact textured surfaces with flexible 3D Gaussian primitives, offering an efficient alternative to purely Gaussian scene representations for structured environments. Existing scene-reconstruction hybrids commonly follow an additive formulation, where Gaussian residuals compensate for errors left by the mesh. However, not every Mesh-Gaussian conflict is caused by missing content: residual geometry errors, occlusion mismatch, texture-projection artifacts, and view-dependent appearance can make the same mesh content consistent in one view but inconsistent in another. We therefore formulate hybrid rendering as a multi-view inconsistency problem and present Gaussolve, a subtract-and-refill framework that selectively dissolves inconsistent mesh content before residual Gaussians refill the revealed regions. We introduce a lightweight Neural Dissolve Field, parameterized by scalar spherical-harmonic Gaussians, to learn where and from which viewing directions mesh content should be dissolved. A continuous transmittance fusion rule progressively exposes residual Gaussians behind the dissolved mesh, preserving their reconstruction gradients and avoiding hard visibility discontinuities. Experiments on structured and semi-structured scenes demonstrate higher visual fidelity with fewer Gaussian primitives and faster rendering. Project page: https://ooosn.github.io/gaussians-can-subtract/
