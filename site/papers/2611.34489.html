Who Is Ego? Scaling Cross-View Camera Wearer Identification and Segmentation beyond Humans

首次收录：2026-11-28 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-25 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

三维几何几何基础模型
arXiv 原文 ↗

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

A first-person (ego) image reveals what a camera sees, but not who or what carries it. Given a synchronized third-person (exo) image, camera wearer identification asks who is ego: which candidate carries the ego camera? We extend this task beyond humans to robots, vehicles, animals, and drones, and broaden its spatial scope (where is ego) from selecting a provided candidate mask to wearer segmentation with absence prediction and mutual camera localization across ego-exo views. We introduce FindEgo-1M, comprising 1.16M real and simulated ego-exo pairs across 12 wearer types, 17 domains, and 111 test settings. Our approach exploits a geometric relation: the ego camera's projection into the exo image, i.e., the epipole, lies on or near its wearer. We introduce EgoRay-ZS, the first method to turn a frozen feed-forward 3D reconstruction model, VGGT-$Ω$, into a training-free solution for wearer identification and segmentation. Its geometric readout ranks candidate masks or segmentation proposals by angular offset and distance to the ego camera, while the relative pose provides mutual camera directions.
