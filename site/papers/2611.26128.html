CryoGaussian: Heterogeneous Cryo-EM Reconstruction Made Explicit with Gaussian Splatting

首次收录：2026-11-21 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-19 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

高斯渲染
arXiv 原文 ↗

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

Single-particle cryo-electron microscopy (cryo-EM) determines macromolecular structures from noisy particle images. However, rare, transient compositional states and subtle, continuous conformational variation are difficult to resolve. We present CryoGaussian, a Gaussian splatting method for heterogeneous cryo-EM reconstruction that fits an explicit Gaussian density representation directly to the particle images through a differentiable rasterizer rendering orthographic line integrals modulated by the contrast transfer function. The Gaussians are initialized randomly, with their number derived from the estimated spatial support of the molecular density and their minimum size bounded by the Nyquist frequency of the particle images. To capture the compositional and conformational variation across particle images, we learn particle-image and per-Gaussian latents that jointly modulate the base position, amplitude, and scale of each Gaussian. Each structural state is then reconstructed by fitting the Gaussians conditioned on its inferred latent.
