Kosmo-GFM: A Production-Ready Geometry Foundation Model

首次收录：2026-11-14 · 代码状态：未核实

3DGS4DGS几何基础模型世界模型可控生成
arXiv 原文 ↗

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

We present Kosmo-GFM, a scene reconstruction system that integrates a geometry foundation model (GFM), a pose optimization engine (PoseEngine), and reconstruction-derived supervision from KosmoWorld. GFM predicts camera parameters, depth, cross-view correspondences, and dynamic 3D points from images and videos. PoseEngine combines these predictions with geometric optimization to recover scene-level camera poses and geometry for downstream 3D and 4D Gaussian Splatting. KosmoWorld generates images and geometric supervision from reconstructed real-world scenes to improve GFM's geometric predictions. On the Sparse and Medium subsets of SpatialBench, GFM achieves the lowest absolute relative depth error and the highest relative-pose AUC at 5 degrees among the compared methods. It also achieves the lowest mean endpoint errors for dynamic points and scene flow across the five evaluated datasets. Across 34 scenes completed by all compared reconstruction systems, PoseEngine achieves the highest scene-averaged pose AUC at thresholds of 1, 3, and 5 degrees, with a cumulative runtime of 14.07 hours, approximately 20% of GLUEMAP's 69.67 hours.
