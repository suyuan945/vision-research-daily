iWAM: Efficient Action Prediction from World Models without Explicit Future Prediction

首次收录：2026-11-25 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-24 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

World-action models (WAMs) build policies on pretrained video-generation models, on the premise that predicting how a scene evolves helps determine how to act. However, generating future scenes is expensive for real-time control. This motivates efficient proxies for future imagination, but how to train such proxies without sacrificing action-prediction quality remains unclear. An effective proxy need not generate a high-resolution frame; it only needs to provide enough information to distinguish between possible futures. This leads to the design principle behind iWAM: rather than denoising an entire future frame, can we condition the action policy on intermediate features from a single video-model evaluation? We show that the answer is yes. Exploiting the reversible dynamics of flow-matching models, we invert each training future into the corresponding latent noise vector. However, a single velocity evaluation at this latent does not necessarily yield informative features for action prediction.
