Most of the Way There: Turning a Strong Video Prior into an Action-Conditioned World Model

首次收录：2026-11-26 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-25 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

世界模型

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

Modern video generators excel at producing visually plausible futures guided by text and image prompts. Yet their emphasis on semantic plausibility does not guarantee fidelity to the physical consequences of specific actions. Action-conditioned world models (ACWMs), in contrast, aim to simulate how the world responds to robot actions, enabling scalable policy evaluation and improvement without costly real-world rollouts. The challenge is not merely to generate a plausible future, but to predict what follows from a specific physical action. As video priors grow stronger, we ask how little adaptation is truly needed to bridge this gap. We introduce Dianjing, a minimal recipe that preserves and unlocks the capabilities of strong video priors through unified physical action conditioning, training concentrated at the high noise levels where motion and layout, and thus the consequences of actions, are decided, and native history conditioning.
