HandWorldBench: A Unified Benchmark for Hand-Centric World Models

首次收录：2026-11-19 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-18 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

世界模型多模态 / 具身视觉
arXiv 原文 ↗

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

Recent advances in world models are opening new possibilities for fine-grained embodied tasks, with hand-object interaction offering a particularly demanding setting. Modeling these interactions involves capturing how hand actions change objects and how the resulting states persist over time. Yet systematic evaluation tailored to these hand-centric capabilities remains underdeveloped. To address this gap, we introduce HandWorldBench, a unified benchmark that systematically evaluates hand-centric world models across human hands, simulated dexterous hands, and real dexterous hands along six dimensions: visual quality, hand fidelity, appearance consistency, instruction following, physical consistency, and temporal coherence. To construct the benchmark, we develop a data curation pipeline that extracts hand-object interaction clips and screens them for quality, yielding 20,500 high-quality egocentric clips from over 600 hours of footage. We then manually select 500 clips covering seven task families for evaluation.
