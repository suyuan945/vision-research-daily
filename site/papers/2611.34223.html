AnyCam4D: Ray-Based 4D Gaussian Rendering for Arbitrary Camera Models

首次收录：2026-11-27 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-25 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

动态重建三维几何高斯渲染
arXiv 原文 ↗

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

Real cameras are not pinholes, yet most dynamic Gaussian splatting assumes they are. Footage is therefore undistorted first, losing field of view or detail, especially with wide-angle and fisheye cameras. Static Gaussian splatting has largely solved this with ray-based rendering, which suits any camera. Native 4D Gaussians, which model motion in space-time, cannot use it directly: each Gaussian's orientation and size cannot be recovered reliably from its covariance. We present AnyCam4D, which trains native 4D Gaussians on original footage by ray-based rasterization, preserving field of view and resolution at about the per-step cost of standard projection. Our key contribution is a numerically stable way to obtain orientation and size: we condition a square root of the 4D covariance. Trained models render through other cameras, with rolling shutter, depth of field or chromatic aberration. With a synthetic 170-degree fisheye camera, training on original images improves peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) by 3.0-8.1 dB. On real footage, AnyCam4D has lower perceptual error than the standard projection adapted to each camera, at comparable PSNR and without camera-specific derivations. Wit
