SprintGS: Eliminating Redundancy for Fast 3D Gaussian Splatting Training

首次收录：2026-11-28 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-25 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

高斯渲染
arXiv 原文 ↗

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) renders photorealistic novel views in real time, but its per-scene training (optimization) remains slow. Recent fast training methods such as FastGS shorten training to a few minutes by reducing the number of Gaussians. Profiling FastGS, we find two costs that remain even in its compact model. First, rasterization is still the largest cost, and redundant work remains in it. Second, the loss computation does not shrink with the number of Gaussians, so its share of the training time grows as the model becomes compact. We present SprintGS, a training framework built on FastGS that keeps the model compact and reduces both costs. For rasterization, scheduled tile culling limits each Gaussian to the tiles where it matters, using a coarse footprint that is annealed to the exact one before training ends; convergence-aware pruning removes converged, nearly transparent Gaussians; and a shuffle-efficient backward pass reduces the data exchanged between GPU threads. For the loss, a fused loss computes the loss and its image gradient in the forward pass without a loss map, removing most of its cost.
