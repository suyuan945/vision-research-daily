Cairn: Object-Centric 3D Trajectory Priors for Steering Frozen VLAs

首次收录：2026-11-26 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-25 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

三维几何多模态 / 具身视觉
arXiv 原文 ↗

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

Vision-language-action models (VLAs) are capable low-level controllers, but they tie where objects go to the layouts they were trained on: when objects are moved or obstacles block the way, they carry them to the wrong place or into collisions. Fine-tuning on new layouts needs robot data for each one and erodes skills the model already has. We introduce Cairn, which keeps the VLA frozen and steers it at test time along learned 3D trajectories of the object in its gripper. A small prior reads point clouds of the scene and the held object, along with its pose when grasped and the instruction, and samples SE(3) trajectories up to its release that lead it around obstacles and turn it to fit through gaps. The prior and the VLA form the two levels of a hierarchical planner in which feedback runs both ways: the VLA's intended motion chooses and reshapes the trajectories, and the chosen one guides the VLA's denoising. Its predicted actions, mapped to the held object's poses through the grasp, are pulled toward the next waypoint, while the VLA's weights stay untouched.
