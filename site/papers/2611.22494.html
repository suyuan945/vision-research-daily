Fast and Strong Image Protection against Diffusion Personalization

首次收录：2026-11-18 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-17 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

Personalized text-to-image diffusion models can reproduce a person from a handful of photographs. Image protection perturbs each image before publication so that fine-tuning on it fails, but it optimizes every perturbation from scratch, so its cost grows with the number of images. We present the protective diffusion model (PDM), which is trained once and samples the perturbation of a new image over many denoising steps. PDM distills an iterative teacher and is then post-trained with the target residual projection (TRP) loss as a reward, a stronger objective than existing ones. PDM outperforms nine baselines on faces and artworks and nearly matches its teacher at a tenth of the cost.
