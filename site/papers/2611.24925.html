Render the Robot, Generate the World: One Video World Model Across Embodiments

首次收录：2026-11-19 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-18 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

世界模型三维几何
arXiv 原文 ↗

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

Predicting the consequences of an agent's actions requires modeling both the body's motion and the environment's response. Most video world models attempt to learn both from numerical actions, even though geometry and kinematics already specify the intended motion. We introduce Doppel, an embodiment-agnostic video world model conditioned on camera-aligned renderings of future body configurations and numerical action trajectories. By explicitly providing the intended motion, Doppel can focus on predicting object motion and interaction-driven changes in the environment. Rendered motion also provides a common visual interface across different embodiments and action spaces. To train on robot datasets without camera extrinsics, we recover camera poses and align rendered trajectories with recorded observations. We jointly train a single shared video backbone and conditioning adapter on eight datasets spanning eight robot and human embodiments, from single- and bimanual robot arms to a dexterous humanoid and human hands and bodies, using about 600 hours of video without embodiment labels.
