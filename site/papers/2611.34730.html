WorldInteract: Controllable Streaming Video Generation for Multi-Material Interactions

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

Video world models aim to create interactive physical worlds that capture how diverse materials move and interact. Yet material-dependent dynamics remain underexplored in controllable video generation, limiting reliable control in scenes with different materials. We present WorldInteract, a streaming video generation framework that decomposes cross-material interaction into material-dependent behavior generation and contact response computation. External pushes and contact impulses are expressed through a shared velocity-increment interface, allowing material behaviors learned from interaction-free videos to be reused in interacting scenes. A physics controller predicts contacts and computes velocity increments from estimated masses and restitution coefficients, while a causal generator executes these increments using learned material behaviors. This separation enables cross-material interaction without supervised contact videos spanning diverse material pairings and contact configurations.
