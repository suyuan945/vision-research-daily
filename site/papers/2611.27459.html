Ego2Ego: Generative Visual Perspective Taking Across Collaborators

首次收录：2026-11-23 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-20 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

Visual perspective taking, the ability to infer what another person sees from their position, is fundamental to human collaboration. However, prior works reduced perspective taking to state estimation, geometric reconstruction, or discrete question answering, whereas existing cross-view video generation methods either inferred the target view from the input video alone or required an external camera that observes the target person. We introduce generative visual perspective taking: Given an ego person's egocentric video, a scene point cloud, and a collaborator's camera trajectory, the task is to generate the continuous egocentric video from the collaborator's perspective. This task is challenging given extreme viewpoint shifts, dynamic occlusions, and sparse visual overlap. We address these challenges with Ego2Ego -- a set of backbone-agnostic geometric modules that provide a pretrained video diffusion model with the 3D scene geometry shared by both interactants.
