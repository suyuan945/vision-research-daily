ViewDreamer: A Robot World Model beyond Fixed Camera Setups

首次收录：2026-11-23 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-20 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

世界模型视频生成三维几何
arXiv 原文 ↗

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

Video generation models are increasingly important for robot learning, yet most generate each camera view separately or tile all views in a fixed layout. Robot demonstrations, however, are captured by synchronized cameras whose number, placement, and order vary across embodiments. We present ViewDreamer, a single world model that handles variable sets of cameras by disentangling per-view generation from cross-view interaction. Each view follows the generation path of a pretrained single-view model, and views interact only through shared slot tokens. We stabilize multi-view training with a curriculum that gradually increases the number of views. Cross-View Slot Agreement uses visual foundation models to group geometrically and semantically similar regions across views into shared slots. On RoboView-Bench, which covers one to four cameras, ViewDreamer achieves the best generation quality among the models evaluated and improves cross-view consistency over independent per-view generation. These gains carry over to our world-action model (WAM) and action-conditioned world model (AC-WM).
