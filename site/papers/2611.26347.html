OffBeat: Multicoset Sampling for 4D Capture Beyond the Camera Frame Rate

首次收录：2026-11-21 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-19 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

动态重建三维几何

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

For 4D reconstruction, a synchronized multi-camera rig records all views at the same timestamps and misses the motion between them. Staggered triggers spread the same frames over more timestamps. However, when the cameras along each row fire one after another, as in 4DSloMo's schedule on the N3DV rig, the row acts as one moving camera that confounds depth with motion. We introduce OffBeat, which models a staggered rig as a spatially distributed multicoset sampler: shifted copies of one sampling grid. From the calibration alone, OffBeat derives camera groups that fire together and their firing order. Since one group records each timestamp, a short-lived primitive can fit an error its cameras share, such as a color offset, and flicker in novel views. We show that a primitive's opacity over time weights all groups equally, for every number of groups, only if it lasts at least one camera frame interval. On Spacetime Gaussians, a minimum lifetime approximates this bound, and a closed-form smoothed box of that length satisfies it exactly, with less flicker than a Gaussian in its place at nearly equal PSNR.
