MoGe-4D: Efficient, Flexible, and Unified Dense 4D Reconstruction and Tracking

首次收录：2026-11-25 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-24 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

Reconstructing dynamic scenes from RGB video requires reasoning about both 3D geometry and motion across viewpoints, yet existing methods either split reconstruction and tracking into fragmented pipelines or face prohibitive dense spatiotemporal decoding costs. We introduce MoGe-4D, a feed-forward framework that unifies geometry and motion prediction via ordered queries of paired allocentric and egocentric dynamic point maps. Each query maps source pixels to a target time simultaneously in both camera frames, resolving relative camera pose in closed form while flexibly serving depth, dynamic registration, and dense 3D tracking. For efficient dense querying, MoGe-4D decouples scene modeling from query decoding: a high-capacity context encoder caches coarse global spatiotemporal features once, while a lightweight query encoder rapidly decodes dense point maps at flexible resolutions.
