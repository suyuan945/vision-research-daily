GeoMVGen: Joint Appearance-Geometry Modeling for Multi-View Video Generation in Robotic Manipulation

首次收录：2026-11-25 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-23 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

Robotic manipulation requires predicting how robot-object interactions evolve over time, encompassing both visual appearance and scene geometry. In multi-camera settings, such predictions need to capture the evolving 3D scene consistently across viewpoints, while supporting controllable generation under different robot actions and camera configurations. Existing video generation models provide strong priors for visual appearance and motion but typically lack explicit geometric modeling. Meanwhile, recent advances in geometry-aware and multi-view generation have explored joint appearance-geometry modeling and cross-view consistency, yet generating temporally coherent appearance and geometry across multiple viewpoints under robotic manipulation conditions remains challenging. In this work, we propose GeoMVGen, a framework for joint multi-view RGB and pointmap video generation in robotic manipulation. Built on a video diffusion transformer, our framework employs modality-specific adaptation modules to capture the distinct characteristics of appearance and geometry, while using cross-modal residual connections to facilitate information exchange between the two modalities.
