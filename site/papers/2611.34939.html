A Mesh for Every Moment

首次收录：2026-11-26 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-25 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

三维几何动态重建高斯渲染

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

We present MEMO (a Mesh for Every MOment), a method that reconstructs the geometry of complete dynamic scenes, captured by multiple synchronized cameras, and extracts a mesh at every frame. Existing dynamic Gaussian splatting methods either model appearance alone or target isolated objects segmented by foreground masks, and all of them recover meshes by TSDF fusion or Poisson reconstruction, whose fixed grids limit the detail they can capture. At the core of MEMO is a per-Gaussian motion model inspired by Gaussian processes, which represents each Gaussian's position and rotation over time as a combination of temporal Gaussian kernels. The model keeps every Gaussian a valid surface element throughout the sequence and carries an implicit representation of the dynamic geometry, so a mesh can be derived at any time from a field defined by the Gaussians. Although common in real captures, large and fast motion poses a challenge that dynamic Gaussian splatting methods usually overlook. To account for it, we design a track-based initialization scheme that fits each Gaussian's initial trajectory to point tracks in closed form.
