Training-Free Acceleration of Multi-View 3D Transformers via Regime-Aware Attention Approximation

首次收录：2026-11-16 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-13 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

Multi-view 3D transformers such as VGGT reconstruct a scene from a set of unposed images in a single forward pass, predicting camera poses, depth and 3D points, but the cost of their global attention grows quadratically with the num- ber of images. Accelerators borrowed from large language models assume that each token attends to only a few oth- ers; on these models they lose accuracy or gain little speed. We study the global attention of three multi-view transform- ers on seven datasets and find that every layer plays one of three roles: it averages over the whole scene, matches points across many images, or anchors on the first image, whose coordinates the outputs use. Building on this anal- ysis, we propose Regime-AWare ATTention Approximation (RAWATTA), a training-free method that runs attention in 8- bit precision and keeps 16-bit precision only in the layers where a worst-case calibration check requires it. Its fast set- ting, RAWATTA-Fast, also approximates attention in a few averaging and anchoring layers, while matching layers and the first image keep all their keys.
