SGAR: Feed-Forward 3D Reconstruction via Sparse Geometry-Guided Attention

首次收录：2026-11-21 · arXiv 提交：2026-11-19 UTC · 代码状态：未核实

摘要（arXiv 元数据）

Feed-forward models have been very successful in 3D reconstruction with ad hoc attention designs that relate all view patches densely. However, the cost of this dense global attention grows quadratically with the number of views, preventing the model from applying to larger-scale reconstruction settings. In this paper, we argue that most dense attention computations are unnecessary from a classical structure-from-motion perspective: 3D structures stem from geometrically matching regions in 2D. We bring this principle to learning-based feed-forward reconstruction with our Sparse Geometric Attention Reconstruction (SGAR) method. In SGAR, each patch token attends to only a small set of patch tokens from other views, chosen by a lightweight learned key selection module trained to predict cross-view 3D proximity of tokens. Thus, dense global attention is replaced by much more efficient geometric-similarity-guided sparse attention. SGAR is a general method that can be applied to different feed-forward models by fine-tuning.
